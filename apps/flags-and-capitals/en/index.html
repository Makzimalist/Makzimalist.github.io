01

Four quiz types

Name the flag, find the flag, know the capital and spot the country on the map – the wrong options in the map quiz are real neighbours.

02

Play the way you learn

Multiple choice or type your answer, the whole world or one continent, 10 questions or all of them. Plus a quiz made of your mistakes for focused practice.

03

Stamps, not points

Every country collects one stamp per quiz type. A wrong answer takes it away again – a country is only mastered once all four are in place.

04

A pocket atlas

All 195 countries with flag, capital, area, currency, official languages and neighbours – plus a zoomable map.

05

Premium, no subscription

The quiz, the atlas and the challenges are free. A one-time purchase unlocks speed rounds, read-aloud and the explorable world map of your stamps – plus a level badge that grows with you: 26 painted frames instead of the plain one.

06

No ads, no tracking

Field Atlas shows no ads, collects no analytics and needs no account. All country data is built in.

07

A new goal every day

The daily challenge changes quiz type and region every day – about every third day is a long round of 20 questions. Plus three daily goals and a weekly challenge.

08

Collect seals, level up

New stamps and rounds earn XP, the level curve never ends and every level has a travel title. Each week you pass raises a seal by one tier – all the way to Legendary.
