2. Overview

“Flags & Capitals” is an Android app with no user account and no server of its own. I do not run a database of user data and I receive neither your name, email address nor your progress. Only your device – and, for purchases and ads, Google – process data.

3. Data that stays on your device

The app stores locally: quiz history and statistics, per-country mastery progress, your settings (appearance, read-aloud) and your premium status. This data does not leave the app and is deleted when you uninstall it. Depending on your device settings, Android may include parts of it in your Google account backup; you control this in the system settings.

The read-aloud feature uses your device’s text-to-speech engine. Whether text is processed locally or by a cloud service depends on the engine installed (usually Google’s); I have no influence on that.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; parent company Google LLC, USA). Google may process: IP address, device and software information, advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). On first start the app asks you via Google’s consent platform (UMP) whether ads may use the advertising ID and personalised selection. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown where legally permitted.

Withdrawal: you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app’s settings. Processing carried out before then remains lawful. You can also delete your advertising ID in Android under “Privacy → Ads”.

Third countries: Google may transfer data to the USA, based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

After a premium purchase, no ads are loaded any more.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Premium purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes payment data; I do not receive it. The app only asks Google which products your account owns in order to unlock them (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google’s privacy terms apply.

6. Permissions

The app needs internet access (for ads) and the Play Billing permission (for purchases). It does not request any other permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone.

7. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Since I do not store personal data about you, I can usually only answer access requests with that fact; claims against Google must be directed to Google. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

I will update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
